LARGE_AMOUNT review policy v1: For a Core LARGE_AMOUNT risk task, use the observed amount and threshold recorded in that risk hit, in the transaction currency, without rounding or currency conversion. If the recorded observed amount equals the transaction amount and is greater than or equal to the recorded threshold, recommend CONFIRMED to retain the risk item for follow-up. This project policy has no monetary exemptions. If the required facts are absent, inconsistent, or cannot be verified, recommend INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE. Do not recommend IGNORED solely because the current threshold differs from the historical threshold. CONFIRMED does not mean fraud, payment, account freeze, or a final business decision. A human reviewer makes the final decision.
